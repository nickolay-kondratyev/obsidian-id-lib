Adversarial review of 085c442 (DocFile domain boundary).

- `peerDependencies.obsidian` was still `*` while `VaultFileContentAccess` now requires `Vault.getFileByPath` (@since 1.5.7). On an older host every ensureDocId died with a bare TypeError, installable without warning. Now `>=1.5.7`, with a manifest.json `minAppVersion` note for consuming plugins (the peer range guards the build, the manifest guards the user).
- `src/obsidian/*` used value-form `import { TFile, Vault } from 'obsidian'`. The vitest tiers have no runtime 'obsidian' since obsidianMock.ts was deleted, so zero-host-import erasure was load-bearing but only implicit in bundler elision. Now `import type` — compile-time guaranteed.
- docs/migrating-to-docfile.md advised `DocFile & Pick<TFile, 'stat'>` to 'keep taking a TFile' — that is the same unsound bivariance the paragraph had just warned about. Replaced with honest guidance.
- DocFile.extension was documented 'Lowercase' though nothing normalizes it; now that the type is structural a consumer could act on that. Documented the exact, case-sensitive dispatch and WHY normalizing would be wrong (Obsidian itself only treats lowercase .md/.canvas as those formats).

Verified: check, check:boundaries, 81 tests, build, check:e2e, and test:e2e (4/4) against real Obsidian 1.12.7.
